smssets

Phish setlists by text message

smssets is a free, personal text message service operated by Kevin McCormack. Text it a date and it replies with the Phish setlist from that night, from Phish.net.

To sign up, text SETLIST to

(312) 579-0081

By texting SETLIST to (312) 579-0081 you agree to receive text messages from smssets in reply to the messages you send. Message frequency varies: one reply for each message you send. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to cancel at any time. See the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

How it works

  • Text SETLIST to (312) 579-0081. You get one message confirming you are signed up.
  • Text a date, such as 12/31/95 or Dec 31 1995. You get one message back with the setlist from that night.
  • That is all it does. Messages are only sent in reply to a message from you.
