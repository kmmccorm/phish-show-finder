smssets Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

smssets is a personal, non-commercial text message service operated by Kevin McCormack (“I”, “me”). You text a date to the smssets phone number and receive the Phish setlist from that date in reply.

Information collected

  • Your mobile phone number and the content of the message you send, which are needed to receive your request and send the reply.
  • If you have asked to use the service, your phone number is kept on a list of numbers permitted to use it.

How it is used

Your information is used only to send you the setlist you requested. Service logs record the last four digits of the sender’s number and the size of the reply. Message content is not stored by smssets.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Information passes through the following providers only as needed to operate the service: Twilio (sends and receives the text messages), Cloudflare (hosts the service), and Phish.net (receives only the date you asked about, never your phone number). Twilio and Cloudflare keep their own records under their own privacy policies.

Retention

Your number stays on the permitted list until you ask to be removed. Provider records are retained according to each provider’s policies.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. To be removed from the permitted list, or to ask a question about your information, contact hello@kmack.me.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
